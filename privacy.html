Privacy Policy

Information We May Collect

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • communicate with users about accounts, purchases, updates, and support
  • protect the platform against abuse, fraud, or misuse

Data Sharing

SoundSwipe does not sell users’ personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers only as needed to operate the platform, including hosting, payments, email delivery, analytics, and security services.
