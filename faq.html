FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A clear guide to discovery, creation, licensing, uploads, purchases, and creator tools on SoundSwipe.

FAQ

What is SoundSwipe?
SoundSwipe is a music discovery and creation platform built for artists and producers. Users can discover beats, songs, and creators, then move straight into creating with the built-in studio.
Who is SoundSwipe for?
SoundSwipe is for artists, producers, and listeners who want a faster, more direct way to discover music and create.
How is SoundSwipe different?
SoundSwipe connects discovery and creation in one place. Instead of finding a beat in one app and recording in another, artists can move from discovery into the studio without leaving the platform.
Can I record over beats in the app?
The mobile app is launching soon. When it goes live, artists will be able to load beats into the built-in studio and record directly inside SoundSwipe.
Do I need to buy a beat before using it?
Not always. Some beats are available for non-profit use, while others require a license before export or publishing.
What does “Free for Non-Profit” mean?
It means the producer allows limited use under the terms shown on the beat. Commercial use still requires the proper license.
What does “License Required” mean?
It means export, publishing, or broader use requires purchasing a license first.
Can I upload my own beats and songs?
Yes. Producers can upload beats, and users can upload songs to their profile.
Can I buy beats through SoundSwipe?
Yes. Beat purchases are processed securely through Stripe.
Do I need a Stripe account to buy a beat?
No. Buyers do not need a Stripe account.
Do producers need Stripe to sell beats?
Yes. Producers must connect Stripe in order to receive payouts.
Does SoundSwipe take a fee on beat sales?
Yes. SoundSwipe takes 10% of each beat sale to support the marketplace and payment flow (for example, on a $100 sale, the seller receives $90 and SoundSwipe keeps $10).
Can I search for beats, songs, and profiles?
Yes. SoundSwipe includes search across beats, songs, and creator profiles.
Can I like, comment, follow, and make playlists?
Yes. SoundSwipe includes social and library features that help users save, organize, and engage with content.
Can I use SoundSwipe on the web?
Yes. The website supports discovery, profiles, uploads, purchases, and account management.
How do I report copyright issues?
Use the Copyright / DMCA contact and reporting process listed on the website.
How do I contact SoundSwipe?
Use the Contact page for support, questions, or account issues.
