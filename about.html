About

Built for artists and producers to move faster.

SoundSwipe connects music discovery, creator identity, beat licensing, and recording in one workflow so artists and producers can move from finding the right sound to building a finished song without bouncing between disconnected tools.

SoundSwipe is a music discovery and creation platform built for artists and producers.

The platform makes it easier to discover beats, songs, and creators without relying on outdated search-driven marketplaces. Artists can find music faster, connect with producers more directly, and move from discovery into creation without leaving the platform.

For producers, SoundSwipe offers a new way to showcase beats, reach artists, and turn discovery into real engagement. For artists, it creates a more direct path from finding the right sound to recording and building a finished song.

By combining discovery, creator profiles, beat licensing, and a built-in studio, SoundSwipe is designed to make the workflow between producers and artists faster, simpler, and more connected.
